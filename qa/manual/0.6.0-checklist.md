# MVS 0.6.0: manual checks (owner)

Hands-on checks for 0.6.0 that need real machines. Tick each box. For a miss, note what you saw (a screenshot helps) and send it to the manager, who routes it to `docs/BOARD.md`.

## LOCK-EV-01: the app shows changes made while it was locked during a call (LR-1 path B, fix #624)

**Why:** when you lock MVS during a call, the call keeps going behind the lock. Things that change while it's locked must show as soon as you unlock, without restarting MVS.

**You need:**
- **Two computers on the same 0.6.0 build:** the PC (owner) and the laptop (member).
- **A test server** the PC owns, with the laptop's account as a member.

### Steps

**Set up the lock**
1. On the **laptop**: Settings → Privacy → **App lock**. Set a passcode you'll remember for this test, e.g. 6 digits.

**Lock during a call**
2. Start a voice call in the test server, with both computers in it (laptop: "Join chat").
3. On the **laptop**: lock MVS with **Ctrl+L**, or "Lock now" in Settings → Privacy → App lock.
   - [ ] The laptop shows "MVS is locked".
   - [ ] The call is still listed: "2 people in the call", "Your mic is off".
   - [ ] The PC still sees the laptop in the call.

**Change something while it's locked**
4. On the **PC** (owner), while the laptop is still locked, **kick** the laptop's account: member list → the laptop's name → "Remove from server" → confirm "Remove".

**Unlock**
5. On the **laptop**: enter the passcode and **Unlock**.
   - [ ] Right away, **with no restart**, the laptop shows "Your account was removed from ⁨server name⁩", and the server is gone from its list.
   - [ ] The call bar is gone: a removed member is no longer in the call.
   - [ ] Nothing still shows the server as if the kick hadn't happened, even after waiting 10 s.

**Clean up**
6. On the PC: Server settings → **Removed accounts** → "Undo removal" for the laptop. Then join again from the laptop with the invite link.
7. On the laptop: Settings → Privacy → App lock → turn the lock **off**: enter the passcode, or use "Forgot it? Unlock with your 12 words".

### Variant (if you have a second member, or the "update needed" case comes up)

Use the same flow, but instead of a kick, make the "An update is required" state or the **update-needed line** (#604) appear while the laptop is locked.

- [ ] After unlocking, the line or notice shows at once, without a restart.

### Report back

- Pass, or the step number that failed.
- What the laptop showed right after unlocking.
- How long until the right state appeared, if it was late.

Don't include passcodes or recovery words in the report.
