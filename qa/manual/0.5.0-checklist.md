# MVS 0.5.0: manual test checklist (owner)

What merged for 0.5.0 on 2026-11-18 and 2026-11-19, as hands-on checks. Tick each box; for a miss, note what you saw (a screenshot helps) and send it to the manager, who routes it to `docs/BOARD.md`.

**Automated coverage.** Each section names it ("Auto:"). The checks there need your eyes: look, feel, wording, direction (RTL/LTR) and real machines.

## Before you start

- [ ] **Two computers** (the PC and the laptop), both on the **same 0.5.0 build**. Reactions and typing need every member of the server on 0.5.0.
- [ ] **A test server** with you as owner and the laptop's account as a member. Use a new server, not one people use.
- [ ] **For the Markdown links:** a third person, or a second account on the laptop.
- [ ] **For member search:** a server with **15 or more** members. The search field appears only from 15. If there's no such server, skip that section and say so.
- [ ] **Languages:** do the run once in Hebrew. Spot-check English on the items marked **(he+en)**.

## 1. Reactions (G-26) and the emoji picker (G-27)

Auto: the core rules (one chip per emoji, 10-per-person cap, refusals, never unread) are in `qa/join-matrix/release050.mjs`.

**Adding a reaction**
- [ ] Hover a message. The toolbar has a smiley-plus button. Click it: the picker opens **under** the message, anchored to it.
- [ ] Pick 👍. A chip "👍 1" appears under the message, **pressed** (accent border and background), because it's yours.
- [ ] Click the chip. Your reaction goes away. Click "+" next to the chips, or "הוספת אמוג'י" in the message's ⋯ menu: the same picker opens.

**Across the two computers**
- [ ] On the laptop, the 👍 appears within a few seconds, **not** pressed (it isn't the laptop's).
- [ ] The laptop clicks it: it becomes "👍 2" on both computers, pressed on the laptop only.
- [ ] A reaction does **not** make the channel unread, show a badge, or notify, on either computer.

**The 10-emoji limit**
- [ ] Add 10 different emoji to one message from one computer, then try an 11th.
- [ ] Expected: "אפשר להוסיף עד 10 אמוג'י שונים להודעה." Nothing else changes.

**Searching the picker**
- [ ] Type לב: ❤️ is found. Then try heart and Heart.
- [ ] Hebrew and English names both work, whatever the app language.

**Picker layout**
- [ ] Recent emoji come first. Below them are the 9 categories with headings.
- [ ] The tabs at the bottom jump to a category, and the current one is underlined.

**Keyboard only (Hebrew UI)**
- [ ] Focus starts in search, and ↓ enters the grid.
- [ ] ← moves **forward** (mirrored in RTL). Home and End go to the row's ends.
- [ ] ↑ from the top row returns to search. Enter picks.
- [ ] Esc closes and returns focus to the button that opened it.

**The composer's picker**
- [ ] The smiley button at the **start** of the composer opens the picker **above** it.
- [ ] A pick is inserted at the cursor, not at the end.
- [ ] Shift+click keeps the picker open for a second pick.

**Narrow window**
- [ ] Narrow the window below about 640 px.
- [ ] The picker becomes a bottom sheet, about 60% of the height.

**Other checks**
- [ ] A deleted message shows no chips and no add button.
- [ ] Emoji look right on Windows: no empty boxes.
- [ ] **(he+en)** Hover a chip. The tooltip shows the emoji and the count, but **no names**. That's a known limit.

## 2. "Is typing…" (G-29)

Auto: the core's events, the off switch and the 6 s drop are in `release050.mjs`.

**Seeing someone type**
- [ ] On the laptop, start typing in a channel the PC has open. Within about 1 s, the PC shows one muted line **above** the composer: "⁨name⁩ באמצע כתיבה…" with three moving dots.
- [ ] The composer does **not** jump when the line appears or disappears.

**When the line goes away**
- [ ] The laptop sends: the line goes **at once**, and the message appears.
- [ ] The laptop types, then clears the box: the line goes at once.
- [ ] The laptop types, then just stops: the line goes within about **6 s**.

**More than one typer** (if you have a third account)
- [ ] Two typing: "X ו-Y באמצע כתיבה…".
- [ ] Three or more: "כמה אנשים כותבים…".

**Never shown**
- [ ] Your own typing never shows on your own screen, including your other computer with the same account.
- [ ] A **blocked** account's typing is never shown.

**The privacy switch**
- [ ] Settings → פרטיות → "הצגת חיווי כתיבה לאחרים" is **on** by default.
- [ ] Turn it off on the laptop and type: the PC shows **nothing**.
- [ ] The laptop still sees the PC typing.

**Other checks**
- [ ] Editing an old message does not show "typing" to others.
- [ ] With Windows "reduce motion" on (Settings → Accessibility → Visual effects → Animation effects off), the dots stand still.

## 3. Markdown in messages and the shortcuts (G-25)

Auto: UI unit tests only. The rendering and the shortcuts need your eyes.

**Send these and check how they look on both computers**

| Send | Expected |
|---|---|
| `**bold**` | **bold** |
| `*italic*` and `_italic_` | *italic* |
| `~~strike~~` | ~~strike~~ |
| `` `code` `` | code, left-to-right even in a Hebrew line |
| ```` ```(new line)two lines of code(new line)``` ```` | a left-to-right code block that scrolls sideways, with a העתקה button (it becomes הועתק after a click) |
| `> a quote` | one quote bar; in a Hebrew quote it sits on the **right** |
| `\|\|spoiler\|\|` | ▮▮▮▮; a click reveals it |
| `https://example.com` | a link; a click asks "Open this link in your browser?" first |
| `2*3*4` and `snake_case_name` | plain, unchanged |
| `# heading`, `- item`, `[text](https://x.y)` | shown **exactly as typed** (not supported in v1) |
| `http://example.com` | plain text, not a link (https only) |

**Spoilers**
- [ ] A hidden spoiler is not copied when you select and copy the message. A link inside a hidden spoiler is not clickable.
- [ ] A notification or a reply preview for a spoiler message shows ▮▮▮▮, not the text.

**Composer shortcuts**
- [ ] Select a word and press Ctrl+B: it's wrapped in `**…**`. Press Ctrl+B again: it's unwrapped. Ctrl+Z undoes it in **one** step.
- [ ] Ctrl+I gives `*…*`, Ctrl+E gives `` `…` ``, and Ctrl+Shift+X gives `~~…~~`.
- [ ] They work with the **Hebrew keyboard layout** active too.
- [ ] With nothing selected, Ctrl+B inserts `****` with the cursor in the middle.

**Enter in a code block**
- [ ] After typing ```` ``` ```` and a line, Enter adds a **new line** instead of sending.
- [ ] After the closing ```` ``` ````, Enter sends.

**The one-time hint**
- [ ] The first time you type a marker, a hint line shows under the composer.
- [ ] ✕ hides it for good, and it doesn't come back after a restart. **(he+en)** Check the Hebrew hint's punctuation and direction.

## 4. Blocked accounts list (G-47)

Auto: UI unit tests only.

**Block someone**
- [ ] Block the laptop's account from the PC: click its name, open the profile card, choose Block.

**The list**
- [ ] Settings → פרטיות → "Blocked accounts" lists it once, with the avatar, the name and the short code.
- [ ] It shows **no** server names.
- [ ] The profile card's block hint has "See blocked accounts". It opens the same list, with focus on its heading.

**Unblock and Undo**
- [ ] Click Unblock. The row goes at once, with no confirm, and "You unblocked ⁨name⁩." shows with **Undo** for 5 s.
- [ ] Click Undo within 5 s: they're blocked again and back in the list.
- [ ] Unblock again and wait 6 s: Undo is gone and they stay unblocked.
- [ ] A fast double-click on Unblock unblocks **once**; it doesn't flip back.

**What a block hides**
- [ ] A blocked account's messages show as "הודעה מחשבון חסום".

**Known gap (not a bug)**
- [ ] Someone you blocked who shares **no** server with you isn't listed yet. It waits for core PRs #502 and #511.

## 5. Removed accounts and undoing a kick (G-53)

Auto: kick, list, the member's not-allowed, undo and rejoin are in `release050.mjs`.

**Kick and the removed list**
- [ ] On the PC (owner), kick the laptop's account from the test server.
- [ ] Server settings → "Removed accounts" (right after Members) lists it: name, code, removal date and "Undo removal".
- [ ] If the page was already open during the kick, the row appears **without** reopening the page.

**What the laptop sees**
- [ ] The server is gone or marked removed on the laptop.
- [ ] The invite link does **not** let it back in.
- [ ] Known gap: the laptop isn't told **why** yet. It waits for #510 and #503.

**Undo the removal**
- [ ] Click "Undo removal": a spinner, then the row goes and a toast confirms. No dialog.
- [ ] A double-click makes **one** undo.
- [ ] The laptop can now join again with the invite link, as a **new** join: it isn't put straight back.

**Other checks**
- [ ] The Invite page says "Removed accounts can't join again with the link." and links to the Removed accounts page.
- [ ] A **non-owner** member does not see the "Removed accounts" page at all.
- [ ] With no removed accounts, the page shows the empty-state text.

## 6. Member search (G-41)

Needs a server with 15 or more members. Auto: UI unit tests only.

**The search field**
- [ ] It appears at the top of the member list only in servers with 15+ members.
- [ ] Typing filters at once, without Enter.
- [ ] Group headers keep their counts, and empty groups hide.

**What matches**
- [ ] The start of any word of a name matches, ignoring case and niqqud.
- [ ] The start of a short code matches, with or without the dash.

**No match and clearing**
- [ ] No match shows "No members match." with "Clear search".
- [ ] Esc once clears the field. Esc again leaves it.
- [ ] Switching server clears the search.

**Typing direction**
- [ ] A Latin query lines up on the **left** in the Hebrew UI, and a Hebrew query on the right (#519).

**Keyboard shortcut**
- [ ] Ctrl+Shift+M jumps to the search field, opening the member list if it's hidden. It also works on the Hebrew layout.
- [ ] Known clash: if your push-to-talk "global mute" key is Ctrl+Shift+M, note which one wins.

**Blocked members**
- [ ] Blocked members still appear in the results, marked as blocked.

## 8. Voice: the IP notice v2, diagnostics, push to talk and sounds

Auto: `qa/voice-e2e` covers joins, mute, leave, kick, push to talk (G74.x) and the join/leave sounds (G64.x). The two-NAT test (#505) covers calls across networks. Not yet run while Docker is down.

**The IP notice v2 (#497)**
- [ ] The first voice join after updating shows the notice **again**, even though you saw the old one. The text says people in the call can see your internet address (IP), and that relay-only turns calls off.
- [ ] Buttons: "הגדרות פרטיות" on the start side, then Cancel and Got it. Tab follows that order.
- [ ] "Privacy settings" opens Settings → Privacy with focus on the relay-only switch. It does **not** join the call.
- [ ] Close Settings: focus is back on the join row.
- [ ] Cancel neither joins nor remembers: the next join shows the notice again.
- [ ] Got it joins, and later joins don't show the notice.

**Connection diagnostics (#496, once it merges)**
- [ ] In a call, click the call bar's ⚙ to open "אבחון חיבור". It's collapsed by default. Open it.
- [ ] A STUN line shows, then a row per person: state, path (e.g. "srflx → srflx"), candidate counts (e.g. "host 1 · srflx 1") and setup time.
- [ ] It shows **no IP addresses or ports** anywhere.

**A call survives Home (dev-ui change)**
- [ ] In a call, open **Home**. The call bar stays and the other side still hears you.
- [ ] Go back to the server. You're still in the same call, and mute or leave still work.

**Push to talk (G-74)**
- [ ] Settings → קול → "לחיצה לדיבור", then pick a key.
- [ ] In a call, the other side hears you **only while the key is held**, and the mute button shows "talking".
- [ ] It stops about 0.2 s after you let go.
- [ ] Switching to another app counts as letting go.

**Join and leave sounds (G-64)**
- [ ] When the other person joins your call, you hear a short **rising** two-note sound. When they leave, a **falling** one.
- [ ] Your own join plays nothing.
- [ ] Turn off "Play a sound when people join or leave the call": silence.
- [ ] While **deafened**, no sound plays.

**Message sound (G-63)**
- [ ] A new message that would notify plays a short quiet blip.
- [ ] During a call it plays only if "during a call" is turned on, which is off by default.

**Echo gate (#440, VC-ECHO-01)**
- [ ] Both computers on **speakers** in one call. Raise one person to 200%.
- [ ] Listen for echo or feedback. This is a 0.5 release gate.
